🎙️ What is a "Stem"? (Vocal vs Beat)

Don't let the word scare you. "Stems" simply means breaking a song into separate tracks!

🎙️ Vocals Track

Isolated Singing

Only the singer's voice. All background music is removed. Perfect for remixes and acapella.

🎸 Instrumental Track

Pure Backing Music

The drums, guitars, synth, and bass with the singer removed. Instant Karaoke backing track!
